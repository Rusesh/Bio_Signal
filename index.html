BioSig

See your signals live

ECG, EMG and EEG from the BioSig board, filtered on the chip and sent to this phone over Bluetooth.

  1. Power the boardThe Bluetooth module's light blinks quickly while it waits.
  2. Pair BIOSIG onceAndroid settings, Bluetooth, pair new device. PIN 1234.
  3. Tap Connect boardChoose BIOSIG from the list. The signal starts in about a second.
Bluetooth isn't available in this browser. Use Chrome 137 or newer on Android, with this page opened from an https:// address. The demo works anywhere.

About

BioSig monitor for the ADS1299 and VEGA AT1051 board. Works with firmware 20.02 and later, over an HC-05 or HC-06 module at 230400 baud. Needs Chrome 137 or newer on Android.
